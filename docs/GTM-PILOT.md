# Kidchemy go-to-market: the first two pilot schools

*Written 2 October 2026. Goal: two private schools (KG to Class 8) running a one-term Kidchemy pilot by the end of November 2026, with both converting to a paid plan for academic year 2027-28.*

This builds on `docs/ROLLOUT.md` (how to run a pilot once you are in). This file is about getting in.

---

## 1. The one-line pitch, per person in the room

| Person | What keeps them up | What you say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Principal** | NEP/HPC expectations, parent complaints after PTMs, standing out at admissions | "Your teachers already notice everything. Kidchemy turns two minutes a week of taps into a Holistic Progress Card for every child, and a one-page sheet for every parent meeting." |
| **Academic / HPC coordinator** | Has to make HPC happen with no tool and tired teachers | "You get the five-domain card, self, peer and parent voice, without asking anyone to write paragraphs." |
| **Correspondent / trustee** | Fees, reputation, admissions | "Show prospective parents a real portrait of a child. The school down the road shows a mark sheet." |
| **Teacher** | Time, PTM dread | "Tap names, not essays. Walk into the PTM with a sheet already printed." |
| **Parent** (via the school) | "Is my child okay? What do I do?" | "The first time someone describes your child instead of scoring them." |

Lead with **HPC + PTM quality**. Belief in "a truer picture of every child" is why the principal *likes* you; HPC and PTMs are why they *say yes*.

**Be accurate about HPC.** CBSE adopted the foundational HPC prototypes in 2023 as "adopt/adapt", not mandatory. PARAKH released preparatory (3 to 5) and middle (6 to 8) cards in 2024. Around 26 states and UTs had adopted or adapted HPC for the early grades by April 2025. In November 2025 CBSE announced it would extend HPC to Classes 6 to 8, with no date given. So the honest line is **"NEP-aligned and ready before you're required to do it"**, not "you are required to do this now". Principals respect a founder who gets this right.

## 2. Who to target first (ideal pilot school)

Pick schools where a yes can happen in two meetings:

- **Private, unaided, English medium, CBSE first** (ICSE and progressive state-board schools second). CBSE schools hear the most about HPC.
- **300 to 1,500 students, KG to Class 8 or KG to 12.** Big enough to care about admissions, small enough that the principal decides alone.
- **Mid-fee band** (roughly ₹40k to ₹1.5L a year). They have a reputation to protect and some budget, and they are not locked into a chain-wide ERP contract.
- **Leadership already "progressive":** activity-based learning, mentions of NEP or "holistic" on their website, a Montessori or play-based pre-primary.
- **In your city, within an hour of you.** You will be physically present at least four times during the pilot.

**Avoid for pilot one:** large chains (procurement takes a term), government schools (procurement and consent are harder; a good phase-2 play via NGOs), and any school mid-way through switching ERP.

### Who inside the school

1. **Principal**: gives the yes on pedagogy.
2. **Academic coordinator or HPC nodal teacher**: your day-to-day owner. Win this person and the pilot runs.
3. **Correspondent / trustee / director**: signs anything that costs money. Not needed for a free pilot, needed for conversion.
4. **One or two teachers**: pick enthusiasts, ideally a Class 3 to 5 class teacher (one teacher, all subjects, sees the class all day).

## 3. Where the leads come from (ranked by conversion)

| Rank | Channel | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Warm personal network** | Parents, relatives and family friends who teach or sit on a school's board. Your own old school. Your friends' parents. Message 30 people today (script A). This is where pilot one almost always comes from. |
| 2 | **Your college** | Faculty in education, humanities or design; the entrepreneurship cell / incubator (they often have school or CSR contacts); alumni who run or founded schools. Ask the E-cell for one intro, not a list. |
| 3 | **Teachers first, principals second** | A teacher who loves the demo will walk you into the principal's office. Find teachers via B.Ed college faculty, teacher communities on LinkedIn and WhatsApp, and Teach For India alumni. |
| 4 | **CBSE Sahodaya school complex** | Regional clusters of CBSE schools that meet for principal workshops. Find your city's Sahodaya, ask a member principal to let you present for 10 minutes at a meeting. One slot = 20 principals. |
| 5 | **Targeted cold outreach** | Build a list of 40 schools from Google Maps + EducationWorld India School Rankings 2026-27 for your city. Find the principal and coordinator on LinkedIn and the school website. Email + LinkedIn + one walk-in (scripts B, C, D). |
| 6 | **Events** | **Didac India, 25 to 27 Nov 2026, BIEC Bengaluru** (walk the floor with printed one-pagers, talk to school owners). Elets World Education Summit, New Delhi, 12 to 13 Oct 2026, is invite-only; apply as a startup or go for the networking only if cheap. Do not exhibit yet. |
| 7 | **Associations (later)** | NISA (budget private schools, 55,000+ schools via state associations) is a scale channel for phase 3 with a cheaper plan. Not for pilot one. |

**Funnel to plan for:** 40 schools listed → 15 conversations → 6 demos → 2 pilots. If you are short after three weeks, the problem is the first sentence of your message, not the number of messages.

## 4. Timing (Indian school calendar)

- **Now to mid October:** Term 1 exams and PTMs are wrapping up. Principals are busy but receptive to "next term". Book meetings for after exams.
- **Late October to mid November (Diwali around this window):** best time for demos and signing a pilot letter.
- **Pilot runs mid November to February.** Teachers sweep weekly; you print PTM sheets for the December or January parent meeting; the term-end HPC is produced in February.
- **January to March:** schools plan the next academic year's budgets and vendors. That is when you convert pilots into paid 2027-28 contracts. Have the impact report ready by mid February.
- **April 2027:** new academic year. Paid rollout to the whole primary section, not the whole school.

## 5. Scripts

**A. WhatsApp to a warm contact**

> Hi [Name], hope you're well! I'm building Kidchemy, a tool that helps teachers create a "portrait" of each child (strengths, how they learn, how they're growing) from a few taps a week, and turns it into the Holistic Progress Card NEP asks for. I'm looking for one or two schools to pilot it free for a term. Do you know a principal or coordinator who'd be open to a 15-minute chat? Here's the site: [link]. Would mean a lot.

**B. Email to a principal (cold)**

> Subject: A Holistic Progress Card for every child in Class [3B], without extra writing
>
> Dear [Principal name],
>
> I'm Arnav, a student at [college], and I've built Kidchemy, a tool that lets a class teacher log what she notices about each child in about two minutes a week (taps, not paragraphs). From that it produces a warm one-page profile for every parent, a printed sheet for every parent-teacher meeting, and a term-end Holistic Progress Card aligned to the PARAKH domains.
>
> I'm looking for two founding schools in [city] to pilot it with one class for one term, free. I set it up in person and sit with the teacher for the first week.
>
> Could I show you a 15-minute demo at your convenience? A sample profile is here: [link].
>
> Warm regards,
> Arnav · [phone]

Follow up once after 4 days, then call the school office and ask for a time to drop off a one-pager.

**C. LinkedIn note (300 characters)**

> Hi [Name], I've built a tool that turns two minutes a week of teacher taps into a Holistic Progress Card and PTM sheet for every child. Looking for 2 founding schools in [city] for a free one-term pilot. Would you be open to a 15-min demo?

**D. Walk-in at reception**

Go between 10:30 and 12:30 on a non-exam weekday, dressed neatly, with three printed one-pagers. Ask for the academic coordinator, not the principal. "I'm a student founder, I've built a free tool for the Holistic Progress Card and parent meetings, and I'd love two minutes to leave this and request a meeting." Leave the one-pager with your number written on it by hand.

## 6. The 20-minute meeting

1. **2 min:** why you built it (your About story, short).
2. **3 min:** the problem in their words. Ask: "How do your teachers prepare for PTMs today? Have you started on the HPC?" Then listen. Write down their exact words; you'll reuse them.
3. **8 min:** live demo, in this order: class sweep on a phone (hand them the phone) → PTM sheet → parent profile → HPC card. Never start with the admin console.
4. **4 min:** the pilot offer (section 7). Show the one-page pilot letter.
5. **3 min:** next step with a date: "Can I meet [teacher name] on Thursday to import the roster?"

### Objections you will hear

| They say | You say |
| --- | --- |
| "Our teachers don't have time." | "That's the whole design. Two minutes a week for a class. If your teacher's sweep takes longer than that in week two, I'll cut prompts on the spot." |
| "Parents only care about marks." | "They care about marks because it's the only signal they get. In the pilot we'll count how many parents open, forward or reply. You'll see the numbers." |
| "We already have an ERP." | "Kidchemy doesn't replace it. It's the qualitative layer your ERP doesn't do. We import the roster from what you already export." |
| "What about data privacy?" | "Children's data belongs to the school. We act only on your instructions, store it securely, share nothing with third parties, and we'll sign a short data agreement. Parents give consent through you." (Have the agreement ready; see section 8.) |
| "Is this AI writing about our children?" | "No. Every sentence traces to something a teacher actually tapped, with dates. A teacher can always answer 'why does it say this?'" |
| "What does it cost after the pilot?" | "The pilot is free. If it meets the goals we agree today, founding schools get a locked founding price for 2027-28." Have the number ready (section 7). |

## 7. The pilot offer

**Scope:** 1 to 2 sections (ideally one primary class + one middle class), 1 to 2 teachers, one term (mid November to February).

**You give:** roster import, in-person onboarding, weekly 15-minute check-in, printed PTM sheets and QR stickers, a term-end HPC for every child, and a 2-page impact report for management.

**They give:** a named owner (coordinator), permission to attend one PTM, 15 minutes of feedback every two weeks, a consent note to parents on school letterhead, and a written "if it works" clause.

**On price.** `ROLLOUT.md` is right that free pilots without an owner convert at about zero. Keep it free (you are a student, and free gets you in), but get commitment instead of money:

- A signed one-page pilot letter with agreed success criteria and the owner's name.
- A conversion clause: "If the criteria are met, the school will consider Kidchemy for the primary section in 2027-28 at the founding price of ₹[X] per student per year."
- **Founding price suggestion: ₹150 to ₹250 per student per year**, locked for two years for the first five schools. School ERP add-ons in India commonly sit around ₹100 to ₹500 per student per year, so this is affordable and still meaningful. Parents always free.

**Success criteria (write them into the letter):**

1. The teacher is still sweeping in week 6 without being reminded.
2. At least 80% of children have 5+ observations by term end.
3. At least 60% of parents open their child's profile; count forwards, replies and screenshots.
4. Parents can pick their own child's profile from three anonymised ones (the unlabelled profile test).
5. The principal or coordinator says they want it for more classes.

## 8. What to have ready before the first meeting

- [ ] Live site deployed with the new landing page and brand (see `docs/SUGGESTIONS.md`, item 1).
- [ ] A **demo school** with a full sample class (20+ children, two terms of observations) that you can show without logging into real data.
- [ ] **One-pager PDF** (front: problem + 3 audience benefits + HPC; back: pilot offer + your photo and contact).
- [ ] **Pitch deck** from `pitchdeck-brief.md` (10 slides; you'll usually show 5).
- [ ] **90-second screen recording**: sweep → PTM sheet → parent profile. Send it after every meeting.
- [ ] **Pilot letter** (1 page): scope, dates, owner, success criteria, conversion clause, data clause.
- [ ] **Parent consent note** template for the school letterhead, in English plus the local language.
- [ ] **Data processing agreement (short)** and a privacy page on the site. Under the DPDP Act and Rules 2025, the core obligations (notice, consent, security, breach reporting, children's data) apply from around May 2027. The educational-institution exemption covers the school's own educational processing; position Kidchemy as the school's processor under its instructions. Have a lawyer glance at the agreement before school two. This is not legal advice.
- [ ] Printed QR sticker sheet and PTM sheet from the demo class, to hand over in the meeting.
- [ ] A simple tracker (Google Sheet): school, city, board, size, contact, role, channel, last touch, next step, date, status.

## 10. What to measure every week

- Teacher sweeps per week, and minutes per sweep (stopwatch, on her phone).
- % of children observed in the last 4 weeks.
- Parent opens, forwards, replies (ask the teacher what parents said).
- Every exact sentence a teacher, parent or principal says about it. These become your website and deck copy.
